# Tennis impact recording

`tennis-forehand.wav` is the decoded public high-quality preview of **Tennis Forehand 2** by **jamesdrake89** (December 1, 2022).

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/jamesdrake89/sounds/662263/
- Public preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/662/662263_4980667-hq.mp3
- License: **Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0 / public domain)**, as stated on the source page.
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- The author describes this as recorded tennis sounds, with traffic and wind beneath the recording.

The app bundles only a 200 ms excerpt from 0.121–0.321 seconds, prepared by `../generate_audio.py`: mono PCM, a gentle 65 Hz high-pass to remove wind/DC, tiny entrance fade, 55 ms exit fade, and peak normalization to 0.8. No synthesized tone, pitch change, added reverberation, voices, or additional effects. The full source is for reproducibility and is not an app resource.

The user's YouTube link (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=atVIPAdrGyE) was a sound reference, not the audio source. No YouTube audio is redistributed.

Build 17 keeps this original recording for clean strikes. A low-pass, shorter version supplies defensive blocks. Clay, grass and hard-court shoe sounds are original procedural textures, generated by [make_surface_audio.py](../Tools/make_surface_audio.py).

## Courtside applause

The two stereo audience cues use the public high-quality previews of three field recordings, all released under CC0. The point cue mixes a short burst of outdoor applause with light cheering. The handshake cue layers a larger audience with a longer natural decay. Both are trimmed, levelled and faded at 32 kHz.

- [Light Applause by ojosdedurazno](https://freesound.org/people/ojosdedurazno/sounds/391326/)
- [Small Auditorium Crowd Applause and Cheering by ElevatorFan2020](https://freesound.org/people/ElevatorFan2020/sounds/859009/)
- [Applause, small audience by nervousneal](https://freesound.org/people/nervousneal/sounds/500977/)

## Winner celebration voices

The archived “Come on!” calls are human recordings, trimmed and levelled as mono 24 kHz PCM. The game uses a silent fist-pump animation.

- The woman’s call uses **COME ON** by Legnalegna55. [Recording](https://freesound.org/people/Legnalegna55/sounds/536896/) · [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- The man’s call uses the opening words of **“Come on guys, let’s go!” — Excited Male** by Iceofdoom, from 2.34 to 2.76 seconds of the public preview. [Recording](https://freesound.org/people/Iceofdoom/sounds/499626/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
